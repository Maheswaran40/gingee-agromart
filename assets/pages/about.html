About Gingee Traveller Agromart

A retail destination for nuts and dry fruits.

Who we are

Gingee Traveller Agromart is a retail destination for nuts and dry fruits, offering a variety of choices for families, individuals and customers looking for delicious everyday snacks. Our goal is to make exploring and purchasing your favourite dry fruits simple and convenient.

Our purpose and commitment

We want every customer to leave with something they enjoy. That means a clear range, helpful answers and packing that is done with care.
